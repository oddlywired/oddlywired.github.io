choice

You accept the status quo because it was chosen.

When presented with a default option, individuals are significantly more likely to stick with it than to choose an alternative.

This default bias simplifies decision-making by reducing the cognitive effort of deliberation. Even in high-stakes areas like insurance, people defer to pre-selected choices regardless of whether they match their needs.

The path of least resistance is usually chosen by others.

Johnson and Goldstein, 2003
