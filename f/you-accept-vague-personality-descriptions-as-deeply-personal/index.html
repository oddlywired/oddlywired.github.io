personality

You accept vague personality descriptions as deeply personal.

Participants in a 1948 study rated a generic, personality-neutral paragraph as highly accurate when told it was written specifically for them.

The Forer effect exploits the human tendency to give high accuracy ratings to descriptions that are actually broad enough to apply to almost anyone. We are inclined to seek meaning in ambiguity, especially when it concerns ourselves.

You are constantly projecting yourself into blank spaces.
