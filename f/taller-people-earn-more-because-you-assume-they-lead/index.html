Taller people earn more because you assume they lead.

A 2004 study found that each additional inch of height correlates with a significant increase in annual earnings.

Researchers analyzed 1,400 people and found that height acts as a proxy for authority. You project leadership qualities onto tall individuals regardless of their actual performance.

You are conditioned to treat physical stature as a indicator of competence.
