Your judgment is skewed by what you saw before.

The contrast effect describes how the perception of an object is changed by its comparison to a preceding object, a phenomenon explored in social psychology regarding attractiveness judgments.

The citation Kenrick and Gutierres (1980) refers to a specific study on social perception (attractiveness), not a general economic or pricing study.
