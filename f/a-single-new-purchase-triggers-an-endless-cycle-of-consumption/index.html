A single new purchase triggers an endless cycle of consumption.

Grant McCracken's 1988 book 'Culture and Consumption' introduced the 'Diderot effect' as a sociological concept, interpreting Diderot's 1769 essay to explain consumer behavior.

It was a theoretical and historical analysis, not a scientific study involving the observation of modern consumption habits.

Your purchase is not an end; it is a signal to keep buying.
