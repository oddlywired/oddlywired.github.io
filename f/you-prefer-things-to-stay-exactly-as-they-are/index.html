decision making

You prefer things to stay exactly as they are.

Status quo bias describes a preference for the current state of affairs, even when changing it would provide a clear advantage.

Change involves risk and uncertainty, which your brain naturally wants to avoid. You perceive the status quo as the safest path simply because you have already survived it.

You are often settling for mediocrity because it feels safer than the unknown.

Where this comes from

Study
Samuelson and Zeckhauser, 1988, Journal of Risk and Uncertainty
