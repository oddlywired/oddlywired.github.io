You are a stranger to your own reasons.

A 1977 study showed that people often invent plausible, but false, explanations for why they chose one object over another.

When people are asked to explain a choice, they search for a logical reason even when the choice was effectively random. They treat this manufactured explanation as the actual reason for their behavior.

You are very good at justifying choices you did not actually make.
