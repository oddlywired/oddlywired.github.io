You assume your future self will share your current desires.

The 2003 paper by Loewenstein, O'Donoghue, and Rabin established the theoretical framework for 'projection bias' that explains why people fail to predict changes in preferences.

The empirical findings regarding hungry participants and food selection were demonstrated in earlier psychological studies, such as Read and van Leeuwen (1998), which the 2003 paper cites to support its model.
