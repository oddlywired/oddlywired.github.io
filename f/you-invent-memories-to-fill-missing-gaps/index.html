You invent memories to fill missing gaps.

In a 1995 study by Loftus and Pickrell published in Psychiatric Annals, 25% of participants reported memories of being lost in a mall after researchers presented them with a false story as if it were provided by their family.

The study appeared in Psychiatric Annals, and the false stories were delivered by researchers using family-sourced information, not by the family members themselves.

Your own memory is the most convincing liar you know.

Where this comes from

Study
Loftus, 1995, Journal of Experimental Psychology
What the check found
The 25% figure is accurate, but the source and the procedural details (who delivered the misinformation) are misattributed or imprecise.
