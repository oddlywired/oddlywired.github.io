You care more about one victim than a hundred.

In a 2007 study, people donated significantly more money to help a specific individual than to a statistical group of victims.

Source: Small, Loewenstein, and Slovic (2007), Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes.

You are wired to respond to faces, not to spreadsheets.
