You only notice your stress when you stop moving.

The incubation effect, as described by Graham Wallas in his 1926 book 'The Art of Thought,' suggests that creative solutions often emerge after you stop consciously focusing on a problem.

Graham Wallas

Giving up on a problem can be the fastest way to solve it.

Where this comes from

Study
Wallis, 1926, The Art of Thought
What the check found
The incubation effect is a valid psychological concept, but the claim misidentifies the author of the seminal text 'The Art of Thought' as Wallis instead of Wallas.

Solid, with limits
