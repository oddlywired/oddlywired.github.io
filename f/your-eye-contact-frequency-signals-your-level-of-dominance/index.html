eye contact

Your eye contact frequency signals your level of dominance.

High-status individuals tend to maintain eye contact while speaking but break it while listening.

Eye contact patterns serve as an unconscious gauge of social rank. Those who perceive themselves as high status exhibit more freedom in controlling their gaze. Conversely, lower-status individuals often show more gaze during listening to signal engagement and monitoring.

Where you look while you listen reveals your unspoken assessment of the hierarchy.

Where this comes from

Study
Dovidio et al., 1988, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
What the check found
Supported by Dovidio et al. (1988). While the finding is empirically documented, the interpretation of the 'Visual Dominance Ratio' as a proxy for status is subject to significant nuance regarding social gender norms.
Confidence
Well replicated
