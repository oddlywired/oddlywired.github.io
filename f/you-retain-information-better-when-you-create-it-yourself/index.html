generation effect

You retain information better when you create it yourself.

Information generated through your own effort is remembered better than information read or heard passively.

This is the generation effect. The cognitive labor of actively constructing an answer forces your brain to create more elaborate neural connections. Passive intake bypasses this encoding, leading to faster forgetting.

You only learn what you work to produce.
