You look to strangers to decide which path is correct.

Asch's (1951) study showed that participants gave incorrect answers in approximately one-third (35.7%) of trials to match the consensus, though the majority of responses remained correct.

The claim uses the word 'often' to imply a majority, when the actual conformity rate was 35.7%.

You are biologically tuned to prioritize group harmony over your own perception.

Where this comes from

Study
Solomon Asch, 1951, Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology
What the check found
While Asch's study demonstrated a notable level of conformity, the majority of answers provided by participants were correct, and the 'social exclusion' motivation is not the sole, undisputed interpretation.
