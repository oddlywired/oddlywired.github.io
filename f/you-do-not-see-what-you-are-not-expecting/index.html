You do not see what you are not expecting.

Highly focused attention can lead to inattentional blindness, where the rate of missing unexpected events ranges from 8% to 83% depending on the difficulty of the primary task.

The 'nearly half' figure simplifies a wide range of results recorded in the Simons & Chabris (1999) study.
