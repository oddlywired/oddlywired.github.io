decision making

You choose the option based on how it is described.

The framing effect describes how the presentation of information influences your perception of the risks and rewards involved.

In experiments, people overwhelmingly prefer a medical treatment described as having a '90% survival rate' over one described as having a '10% mortality rate,' despite the odds being identical. The language used triggers different emotional reactions that override rational calculation. You are reacting to the frame, not just the fact.

The context provides the meaning you act upon.
