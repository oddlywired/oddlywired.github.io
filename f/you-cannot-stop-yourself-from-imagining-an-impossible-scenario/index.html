imagination

You cannot stop yourself from imagining an impossible scenario.

Counterfactual thinking is your brain's persistent attempt to simulate 'what if' scenarios to understand outcomes.

Even when an event is irreversible, you mentally construct alternatives. This simulation serves an adaptive purpose: it helps you learn from potential mistakes. However, it also traps you in a cycle of reliving moments that can no longer change.

You are hardwired to replay reality with different variables.
