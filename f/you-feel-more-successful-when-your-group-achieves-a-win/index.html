social identity

You feel more successful when your group achieves a win.

Basking in reflected glory is the psychological attempt to maintain self-esteem by aligning yourself with successful others.

Researchers observed that university students were more likely to wear school-branded apparel on campus the Monday after their football team won a game. They also used the pronoun 'we' to describe the team's success but 'they' to describe the team's losses.

You use other people's achievements to bolster your own identity.
