decision making

You misremember why you chose what you chose.

Once a decision is made, you unconsciously amplify the positive aspects of your choice and ignore its downsides.

This bias ensures your self-image remains consistent with your actions. Research shows that people rewrite their past preferences to align with the options they eventually picked.

You justify your past self to keep your current self comfortable.

Where this comes from

Study
Mather, Shafir, and Johnson (2000)
What the check found
The cited study (Mather, Shafir, & Johnson, 2000) specifically examines how people misremember the features of choices they have made to make the chosen option seem superior to the rejected one.
