You explain your errors with context, others with character.

The actor-observer asymmetry is a hypothesis suggesting that people attribute their own actions to situational factors while blaming others' actions on personality; however, this effect has not been empirically validated.

Meta-analyses of research spanning 1971 to 2004 found no consistent support for the actor-observer asymmetry.

You are always the victim of circumstance, and they are always the victim of their own choices.

Where this comes from

Study
Jones and Nisbett, 1971
What the check found
While Jones and Nisbett (1971) proposed the hypothesis, it has failed to replicate consistently in meta-analyses, and its status as a robust cognitive bias is widely contested in modern psychology.
