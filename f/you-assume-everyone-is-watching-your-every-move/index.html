You assume everyone is watching your every move.

The spotlight effect is the tendency to overestimate how much others notice your appearance or your behavior.

When you worry about a minor stain on your shirt or a stumble in your speech, you believe others are tracking these details. In reality, most people are entirely focused on their own internal experiences and concerns. You are the star of your own mental movie, but everyone else is the star of theirs.

You are rarely as memorable as you fear you are.
