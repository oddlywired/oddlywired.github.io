You create your own excuses before you even start.

Self-handicapping is the strategy of creating obstacles to performance to protect your self-esteem if you happen to fail.

In a 1978 study, participants chose to consume a drug that would likely impede their performance on a test because they were anxious about their ability. It is easier to blame the obstacle than to face a lack of skill.

Failing is easier when you ensure it was never your fault.
