attention

Your attention is limited to one object.

When you are highly focused on a specific task, you may experience 'inattentional blindness,' where you fail to notice unexpected stimuli in your visual field.

The brain does not become blind to everything else in the scene; it only fails to consciously process unexpected or irrelevant stimuli while under high cognitive load.

Your focus is both your greatest strength and your most significant vulnerability.

Where this comes from

Study
Mack & Rock, 1998, MIT Press
What the check found
The term 'attentional tunnel' is often used in aviation psychology, while Mack & Rock (1998) authored the seminal 'Inattentional Blindness' book regarding unexpected stimuli, not complete visual negation.
