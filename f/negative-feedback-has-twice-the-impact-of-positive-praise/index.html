negativity bias

Negative feedback has twice the impact of positive praise.

In studies of social interaction and performance, humans weigh negative information significantly more heavily than equivalent positive data.

Evolutionary models suggest this evolved to prioritize threat detection over reward seeking. While one compliment feels good, one criticism commands your focus for hours because your brain categorizes danger as more urgent than growth.

Your mind is built to obsess over flaws rather than celebrate wins.
