Your brain prioritizes threatening information over positive feedback.

A 2001 review demonstrated that negative events have a stronger and longer-lasting impact on the human psyche than positive ones.

This is the negativity bias. Evolutionarily, it was more important to notice a predator than a patch of flowers. Today, this manifests as our obsession with criticisms over compliments.

You have to work harder to remember the good than the bad.

Where this comes from

Study
Baumeister et al., 2001
What the check found
The claim correctly identifies the 2001 review paper 'Bad Is Stronger Than Good' by Baumeister, Bratslavsky, Finkenauer, and Vohs, which widely established the negativity bias in psychological literature.
