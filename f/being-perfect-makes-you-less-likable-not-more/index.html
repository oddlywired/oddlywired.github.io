likability

Being perfect makes you less likable, not more.

A competent person becomes more attractive and relatable when they commit a minor, clumsy error.

A 1966 study found that observers gave the highest likability ratings to a high-achieving candidate who spilled coffee during an interview. The mistake humanizes the individual.

Your flaws are the bridge to connection.

Where this comes from

Study
Aronson, Willerman, and Floyd, 1966, Psychonomic Science
What the check found
The study (Aronson, Willerman, & Floyd, 1966) is a classic in social psychology. The effect is real but strictly moderated by the perceived baseline competence of the subject.
