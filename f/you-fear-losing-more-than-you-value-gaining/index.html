risk

You fear losing more than you value gaining.

Prospect theory demonstrates that losses are weighted more heavily than equivalent gains.

The '2:1' ratio is a commonly cited empirical estimate from later studies and does not originate as a fixed value in the 1979 Tversky and Kahneman publication.

You choose safety not because it is good, but because it is safe.

Where this comes from

Study
Tversky and Kahneman, 'Prospect Theory', 1979
What the check found
While the qualitative claim aligns with the core of prospect theory, the '2:1' figure is an oversimplified summary estimate from later literature, not a specific constant defined in the 1979 study.
