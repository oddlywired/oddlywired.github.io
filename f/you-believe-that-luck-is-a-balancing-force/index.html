You believe that luck is a balancing force.

A 1971 study by Tversky and Kahneman, titled 'Belief in the Law of Small Numbers,' found that people expect short-term streaks of randomness to correct themselves, even when the odds never change.

The study was titled 'Belief in the Law of Small Numbers,' not 'Gambler's Fallacy.'

You see a pattern where there is only probability.
