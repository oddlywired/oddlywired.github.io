You see patterns that do not actually exist in data.

Apophenia describes the human tendency to perceive meaningful connections between unrelated information.

The human brain is an efficiency machine designed to spot threats, leading it to over-index on randomness. If you flip a coin and get heads five times, you feel a pattern emerging, even though the coin has no memory.

Your mind finds order because the alternative is chaos.
