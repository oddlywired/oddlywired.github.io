You value things more once you possess them.

The mere ownership effect is the finding that items are rated as more attractive simply because they are labeled as belonging to you.

In studies, participants rated objects as having higher monetary and aesthetic value the moment they were assigned ownership. This occurs even when the ownership is arbitrary or temporary. The brain integrates possessions into the self-concept, causing an automatic boost in value.

You are emotionally attached to objects that you did not want yesterday.
