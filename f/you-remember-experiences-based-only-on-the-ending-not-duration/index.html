memory

You remember experiences based only on the ending, not duration.

In a 1993 study, participants rated an uncomfortable experience more favorably if the end was less painful, regardless of total time.

Subjects kept their hands in freezing water for 60 seconds. A second trial lasted 90 seconds, with the final 30 seconds being slightly warmer. Participants preferred to repeat the longer trial because the ending felt less severe.

The final moments of your day or project dictate your memory of the whole.
