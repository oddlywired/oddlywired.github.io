memory

Your brain blocks out memories to clear the way.

Proactive interference occurs when your older memories disrupt your ability to recall newer information.

When you try to learn new patterns, your brain struggles if you already have deeply ingrained, competing information in that same domain. The past essentially crowds out the present. This creates a functional barrier where old habits effectively block new learning.

Your existing knowledge can become a wall, not a foundation.
