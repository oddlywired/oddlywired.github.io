affect heuristic

You decide based on feelings, then justify with logic.

A 2000 study by Finucane, Alhakami, Slovic, and Johnson demonstrated that people evaluate the risks and benefits of an activity based on their affective associations.

The year of the study is 2000, not 2004.

You know what you want before you know why.

Where this comes from

Study
Paul Slovic, 2004, Affect Heuristic
What the check found
The core finding was published in 2000. While Paul Slovic did write influential pieces on the affect heuristic in 2004, the claim erroneously dates the original discovery to that year.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
