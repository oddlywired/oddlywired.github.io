You are more motivated on the first of January.

A 2014 study found that people seek more 'fresh start' opportunities during temporal landmarks like birthdays or new years.

Temporal landmarks allow you to mentally categorize your life into distinct chapters. By isolating a past version of yourself from the current one, you feel more capable of pursuing new goals. These moments provide a psychological break that helps you relegate old failures to the past.

We crave a clean slate to hide our history from ourselves.

Where this comes from

Study
Dai, Milkman, and Riis, 2014, Management Science
