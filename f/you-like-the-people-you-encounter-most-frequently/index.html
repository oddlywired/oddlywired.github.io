You like the people you encounter most frequently.

Physical proximity is a stronger predictor of friendship than shared interests.

In a 1950 study of university housing, residents were significantly more likely to befriend those who lived on the same floor or near stairwells. Frequent, unplanned interactions reduce perceived distance and increase rapport. You gravitate toward the path of least social resistance.

Your closest relationships are often products of geography.
