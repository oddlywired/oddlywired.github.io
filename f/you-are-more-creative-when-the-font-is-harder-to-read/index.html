You are more creative when the font is harder to read.

Cognitive disfluency—the experience of difficulty in processing information—can trigger more deliberate, analytic thinking.

Studies show that when people encounter information in a difficult-to-read font, they slow down their cognitive processing. This effortful state reduces reliance on intuitive gut reactions and forces a deeper engagement with the material at hand.

Efficiency feels good, but ease is often the enemy of careful thought.

Where this comes from

Study
Alter et al., 2007, Journal of Experimental Social Psychology
What the check found
The cited study exists and is widely discussed in psychology, but empirical support in the field has shown that the effect is not always robust or replicable across different experimental conditions.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
