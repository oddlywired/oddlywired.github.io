social dynamics

You assign human traits to objects to satisfy a need for connection.

A 2008 study found that individuals experiencing social exclusion were more likely to attribute human-like intentions to inanimate gadgets.

When your need to belong goes unfulfilled, your brain actively seeks out agency in the environment to compensate for the loss of human interaction. It is a psychological survival mechanism to maintain a sense of relatedness.

Your attachment to technology can be a proxy for social hunger.
