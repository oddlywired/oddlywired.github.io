You feel less guilty when you choose inaction.

Humans assign more moral weight to active harm than passive neglect.

When faced with a tragic outcome, people judge the person who acted harshly more severely than the one who sat by and did nothing. This holds even when the inaction leads to the exact same negative result. You view the path of least resistance as inherently safer for your moral standing.

You prioritize your conscience over the actual outcome.
