You learn more by studying in intervals than in blocks.

The spacing effect, first studied systematically by Hermann Ebbinghaus in 1885, demonstrates that information is retained longer when study sessions are distributed over time rather than massed.

The claim is overstated because it uses modern educational vernacular and claims it as an Ebbinghaus finding.

Consistency is a better teacher than intensity.

Where this comes from

Study
Ebbinghaus, 1885
What the check found
Ebbinghaus did establish the efficacy of distributed practice in 1885, but the 'illusion of competence' phrasing is a modern interpretation, not a quote from his 19th-century text.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
