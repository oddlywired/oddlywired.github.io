You assume attractive people are also morally good.

While Thorndike identified the 'halo effect' as a general cognitive error in 1920, the systematic experimental finding that physical attractiveness correlates with perceived intelligence and morality was established later.

The specific link between attractiveness and perceived personality traits is primarily attributed to the 1972 study 'What is Beautiful is Good' by Karen Dion, Ellen Berscheid, and Elaine Walster.

You judge strangers by their face before you judge them by their actions.
