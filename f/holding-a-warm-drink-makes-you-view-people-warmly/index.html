Holding a warm drink makes you view people warmly.

A 2008 study reported that physical warmth might increase interpersonal warmth, but subsequent independent, higher-powered studies failed to replicate this effect.

The finding is not currently considered a reliable, established psychological phenomenon.

You interpret character based on the physical sensations you feel.
