self control

You consistently overestimate your ability to handle immediate temptation.

The restraint bias is a pattern where individuals overestimate their self-control and consequently put themselves in situations they cannot manage.

In a 2009 study, smokers were significantly more likely to overestimate their ability to resist cravings when placed in environments filled with smoking cues. By assuming you can manage temptation, you fail to build the necessary distance from it. You are rarely as disciplined as you imagine in the moment.

True discipline involves avoiding the test, not just passing it.
