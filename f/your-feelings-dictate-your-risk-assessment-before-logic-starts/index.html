Your feelings dictate your risk assessment before logic starts.

The affect heuristic is the process of using current emotions to make rapid decisions instead of evaluating complex data.

Research demonstrates that when people feel positive about an activity, they judge its risks as lower and its benefits as higher, regardless of actual statistical safety profiles.

You justify your decisions with logic after your feelings have already made the choice.
