decision making

You continue painful projects to avoid admitting loss.

Individuals invest more time and resources into a failing project simply because they have already invested in it.

This is the sunk cost fallacy, where the investment of past effort becomes a psychological barrier to cutting losses. Even when the future prospects of the project are poor, the desire to justify the previous expenditure keeps you committed.

You treat the past as a reason for future mistakes.
