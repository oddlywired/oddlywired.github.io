You judge your current life against those performing better.

The term 'upward social comparison' emerged from later developments in social comparison theory, building upon but distinct from Festinger's original 1954 work.

Festinger's 1954 theory centered on the innate drive to compare oneself with others who are similar in ability or opinion to improve the accuracy of self-evaluation.

You use others as a benchmark while ignoring the variables they hide.
