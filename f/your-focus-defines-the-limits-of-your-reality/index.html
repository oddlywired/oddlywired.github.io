attention

Your focus defines the limits of your reality.

The bottleneck model of attention suggests you process only a fraction of available sensory input.

You cannot attend to every input at once; your brain filters information early to prevent overload. This selective process is why you can focus on a conversation in a crowded room but fail to notice the background changing.

You only ever experience a curated version of the world.

Where this comes from

Study
Donald Broadbent, 1958, Filter Model of Attention
What the check found
The claim accurately reflects the core concept of Donald Broadbent's 1958 Filter Model, which defined selective attention as a bottleneck process filtering information before semantic analysis.
