cognition

Your performance drops when you fear confirming a stereotype.

The awareness of a negative stereotype regarding your group can cause an involuntary decline in performance.

In a 1995 study, students performed worse on a test when it was labeled as a measure of intellectual ability. When the test was framed as a neutral puzzle, the performance gap vanished. Anxiety consumes the cognitive capacity you need for the task.

The environment acts as a glass ceiling on your potential.
