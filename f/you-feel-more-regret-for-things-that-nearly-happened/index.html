You feel more regret for things that nearly happened.

Counterfactual thinking causes stronger emotional reactions to 'near misses' than to outcomes that were never possible.

Studies of Olympic medalists show that bronze medalists appear happier than silver medalists. The silver winner focuses on the gold they barely missed, while the bronze winner focuses on the possibility of not medaling at all.

The brain creates more pain when it can easily imagine an alternative reality.
