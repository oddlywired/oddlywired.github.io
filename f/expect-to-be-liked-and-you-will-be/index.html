social interaction

Expect to be liked, and you will be.

A 1986 study showed that people who were led to believe they were liked by others were more likely to initiate friendly interactions.

Your expectation acts as a self-fulfilling prophecy. Because you believe the social outcome is positive, you exhibit warmer, more open behavior, which naturally elicits a positive response from the person you are interacting with.

The kindness you receive is often a mirror of what you expected to find.
