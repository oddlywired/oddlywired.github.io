You expect your future self to work perfectly.

A 1994 study found that people consistently underestimate how long tasks take, even when they know their own history of being late.

You focus on the ideal scenario and ignore the inevitable complications. You treat your upcoming schedule as if it exists in a vacuum where nothing goes wrong.

Your future plans ignore your own past behavior.

Where this comes from

Study
Buehler, Griffin, and Ross, 1994, Planning Fallacy
What the check found
The 1994 study is the seminal work on the planning fallacy. The claim correctly identifies the core mechanism (ignoring past history in favor of the current task's ideal scenario).
Confidence
Solid, with limits
