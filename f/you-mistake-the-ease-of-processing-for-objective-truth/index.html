You mistake the ease of processing for objective truth.

People rate statements as more accurate simply because the font is easier to read.

Studies show that claims written in high-contrast, clear fonts are judged as more likely to be true than those in difficult-to-read text. Fluency in reading is often misinterpreted by the brain as familiarity or reliability.

If you struggle to read it, you trust it less.

Where this comes from

Study
Reber and Schwarz (1999), Effects of Perceptual Fluency on Judgments of Truth
What the check found
Reber and Schwarz (1999) is the seminal paper on this effect, demonstrating that perceptual fluency (ease of processing) is often misattributed to the truthfulness of the statement.
