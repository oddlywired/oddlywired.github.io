You perceive members of other groups as all identical.

Outgroup homogeneity is the tendency to see individuals in other groups as having fewer unique traits than people in your own group.

You are intimately aware of the nuances and differences among your friends, but you categorize outsiders using broad, flat stereotypes. This happens because you have limited exposure to the internal diversity of other social circles.

You fail to see the individual because you are only looking at the label.

Where this comes from

Study
Quattrone and Jones, 1980
What the check found
The definition accurately reflects the outgroup homogeneity effect as established by Quattrone and Jones in their 1980 study.
