Nostalgia actually makes you feel physically warmer.

A 2012 study found that participants induced to feel nostalgic reported significantly higher perceived ambient temperatures.

Nostalgia serves as a psychological resource to combat loneliness and cold, activating neural pathways associated with comfort. The brain uses these memories to simulate a sense of security when the environment feels harsh.

You are using the past to regulate your current physical comfort.
