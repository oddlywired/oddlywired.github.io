You view your own success as someone else's loss.

Belief in a zero-sum world leads people to interpret social and economic gains as fixed pots where one person's progress requires another's failure.

Studies show this mindset persists even when evidence demonstrates that mutual cooperation increases resources for everyone. It acts as a rigid mental shortcut to navigate competition, regardless of the objective reality.

You might be fighting for a bigger slice of a pie that is actually growing.
