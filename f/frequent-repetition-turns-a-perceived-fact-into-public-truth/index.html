social dynamics

Frequent repetition turns a perceived fact into public truth.

The availability cascade explains how a belief gains plausibility through its self-reinforcing repetition in social and media circles.

Research indicates that when a claim is repeated across many channels, it shifts from an individual opinion to a collective fact, regardless of the original supporting evidence.

Social truth is often just the loudest argument heard most frequently.
