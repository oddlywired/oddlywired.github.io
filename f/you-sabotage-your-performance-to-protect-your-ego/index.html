You sabotage your performance to protect your ego.

Self-handicapping is the tendency to create obstacles to your own success so you have a ready-made excuse for failure.

By placing hurdles in your own path, such as staying up late before a presentation, you ensure that failure reflects a lack of effort rather than a lack of ability. This shields your self-esteem from the possibility of innate incompetence. It preserves your perception of potential.

You prefer to be seen as lazy rather than incapable.
