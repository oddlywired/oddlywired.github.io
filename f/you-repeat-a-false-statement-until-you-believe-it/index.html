You repeat a false statement until you believe it.

The illusory truth effect describes the tendency to believe repeated information is more credible, a phenomenon first established by Hasher, Goldstein, and Toppino in 1977.

While the 1977 study established the effect, the finding that it persists even when subjects have prior knowledge to the contrary was identified in later research, such as Fazio et al. (2015).

The more you hear a lie, the more your brain treats it as fact.

Where this comes from
