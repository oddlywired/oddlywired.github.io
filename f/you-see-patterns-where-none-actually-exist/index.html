pattern recognition

You see patterns where none actually exist.

Illusory correlation causes you to perceive a relationship between two variables even when they are statistically unrelated.

In 1967, participants observed pairs of words and consistently claimed a connection existed based on semantic meaning, despite the pairing being completely random. You subconsciously filter data to confirm these false connections.

Your desire for coherence often forces a story onto random noise.
