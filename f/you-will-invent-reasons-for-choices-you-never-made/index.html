self deception

You will invent reasons for choices you never made.

A 2005 study showed participants would justify why they chose a face they did not actually pick during a manipulated swap.

Your brain prefers narrative consistency over objective accuracy. When faced with an outcome, you construct a retrospective justification to explain your preference. You remain unaware that your choice was manipulated because you trust your internal reasoning process blindly.

Your convictions are often a retrospective fabrication.

Where this comes from

Study
Hall and Johansson, 2005
What the check found
The study, 'Choice Blindness' by Lars Hall and Petter Johansson (Science, 2005), is a landmark paper in psychology demonstrating that people often confabulate justifications for choices they did not actually make.
Confidence
Well replicated
