Your brain reads the shape of words, not every letter.

A 1969 study demonstrated that individuals identify letters more accurately when they appear within a whole word than in isolation.

Context allows your brain to bypass the slow process of analyzing individual characters. You recognize the holistic pattern, which then facilitates the recognition of the component letters. The word is the unit of meaning, not the alphabet.

You see the world through the shortcuts you have perfected.
