decision making

You perform differently when the task requires effort.

The effort-accuracy trade-off shows that you adjust your cognitive effort based on how much accuracy you believe the task requires.

When you perceive a task as 'low stakes,' you automatically shift to lower-effort processing strategies. You rarely use your full cognitive capacity unless the environment forces you to do so.

You are a cognitive miser by default, not by choice.

Where this comes from

Study
Payne et al., 1988
What the check found
The seminal paper is Payne, J. W., Bettman, J. R., & Johnson, E. J. (1988), 'Adaptive Strategy Selection in Decision Making'. The claim accurately reflects the core thesis regarding the effort-accuracy trade-off.
