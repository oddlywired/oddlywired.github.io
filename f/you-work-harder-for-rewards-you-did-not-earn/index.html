value

You work harder for rewards you did not earn.

In the 2011 'IKEA Effect' study, participants were willing to pay significantly more—in one experiment, 63% more—for products they assembled themselves compared to those assembled by others.

The 20% figure cited is not supported by the original paper, which found a much larger effect size for participants who fully assembled products.

Ownership is a feeling you can trigger with small amounts of effort.

Where this comes from

Study
Norton, Mochon, and Ariely, 2011, Journal of Consumer Psychology
What the check found
The direction of the claim is correct, but the specific number is inaccurate and understates the effect size reported in the primary research.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
