decision making

You are statistically likely to choose the pre-selected option.

A 2003 study of organ donation found that countries with an opt-out policy had nearly 100% participation compared to 15% in opt-in countries.

People rarely exert the effort to change a default setting provided by an institution. We treat the pre-selected choice as a recommendation rather than a random assignment. This inertia dictates everything from software settings to retirement savings.

The choices you make are often just the paths of least resistance.
