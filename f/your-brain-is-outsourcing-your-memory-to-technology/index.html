Your brain is outsourcing your memory to technology.

A 2011 study found that people are less likely to remember facts if they believe they can search them later.

When participants were told a computer would save their notes, their recall of the actual information dropped significantly. The brain treats the computer as an external hard drive, prioritizing the location of the info over the content itself. This shift is sometimes referred to as the Google effect.

You are beginning to remember where to look, not what you know.
