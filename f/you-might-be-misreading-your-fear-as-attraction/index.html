You might be misreading your fear as attraction.

The misattribution of arousal occurs when your brain misidentifies the source of physiological excitement.

In a 1974 study, men who crossed a high, anxiety-inducing suspension bridge were more likely to call a female interviewer afterward than those who crossed a low, stable one. Your body registers high pulse and palms as 'feeling,' then labels it based on the person nearby.

Your emotions are often just physiological signals in search of a label.

Where this comes from

Study
Dutton and Aron, 1974, The Bridge Study
