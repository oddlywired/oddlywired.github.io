decision

Your first impression of a number dictates your final choice.

Providing an initial, arbitrary value significantly shifts your estimation of a subsequent, unrelated number.

Experiments show that even obviously irrelevant numbers influence how much people guess for things like house prices or quantities. Your mind lacks an absolute internal scale and relies on the anchor provided.

You are easily biased by the first figure you see.
