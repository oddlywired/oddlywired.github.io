memory

Remembering to remember is a distinct, fragile process.

Research shows that prospective memory—the ability to perform a future intention—relies on different brain mechanisms than retrospective memory.

While standard memory is about pulling from the past, prospective memory requires you to keep an intention active while attending to a completely different task. It is highly susceptible to disruption by environmental distractions.

You are better at keeping the past than planning the future.

Where this comes from

Study
McDaniel and Einstein, 2007, Prospective Memory: An Overview and Synthesis
What the check found
The distinction is a foundational concept in the cited book by McDaniel and Einstein. The claim is accurate in spirit, though scientific nuance suggests significant overlap in neural architecture.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
