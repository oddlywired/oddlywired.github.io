You create stories to fill in missing information.

People consistently invent coherent motives for their random physical actions after the fact.

When participants' brains were stimulated to move a limb without their intention, they reported a conscious desire to perform the movement. The mind hates gaps in agency and generates an explanation to make sense of the behavior.

You are a narrator constantly explaining your body to yourself.
