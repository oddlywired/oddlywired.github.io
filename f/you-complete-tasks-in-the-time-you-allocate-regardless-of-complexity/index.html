You complete tasks in the time you allocate, regardless of complexity.

Parkinson's Law states that work expands to fill the time available for its completion.

Observations in organizational management show that the perceived urgency of a task is dictated by the deadline set, rather than the intrinsic labor required. If you give yourself a week to write a report, you will use the week. If you halve the time, you will usually find a way to finish regardless.

Your timeline is a constraint you set, not a requirement of the task.

Study
Parkinson, 1955, The Economist
What the check found
