You do not know why you reached that conclusion.

The confabulation effect occurs when you provide a logical reason for an action that was actually caused by a subconscious prime or hidden influence.

When the true reason for a choice is hidden, your brain does not accept 'I don't know' as an answer. Instead, it constructs a plausible, logical-sounding narrative to explain your behavior to yourself.

Your brain is a better storyteller than it is an observer.

Where this comes from

Study
Nisbett and Wilson, 1977
What the check found
The claim accurately describes the finding of the 1977 paper, even if the label 'confabulation' was popularized later to describe this specific behavior of manufacturing reasons for unconscious choices.
