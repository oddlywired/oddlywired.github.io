trust

You trust people with easier names more than others.

A 2012 study demonstrated that names easy to pronounce are judged as more trustworthy.

Ease of processing translates into a feeling of familiarity. You interpret this psychological fluency as a signal of safety and truthfulness, while harder names trigger subtle hesitation.

You are biased against people whose names require effort to say.

Where this comes from

Study
Laham et al., 2012, Journal of Experimental Social Psychology
What the check found
The study 'The name-pronunciation effect: Why easier to pronounce names are more trustworthy' by Laham, Koval, and Alter was published in the 2012 Journal of Experimental Social Psychology.
