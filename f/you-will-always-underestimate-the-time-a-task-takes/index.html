You will always underestimate the time a task takes.

People consistently predict they will complete tasks faster than they actually do.

In a 1994 study, students were asked to estimate their thesis completion dates; their predictions were almost always optimistic, ignoring their past performance trends. You focus on the best-case scenario for your schedule.

Your calendar is a record of hopes, not your history.
