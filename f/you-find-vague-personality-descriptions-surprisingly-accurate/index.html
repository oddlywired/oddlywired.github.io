You find vague personality descriptions surprisingly accurate.

The Forer effect is the tendency for people to accept broad personality assessments as uniquely applicable to them.

When participants are given a generic paragraph of statements meant to sound personal, they rate it as highly accurate. The language relies on the human tendency to look for patterns and assume deep meaning in platitudes. Your need to understand yourself makes you susceptible to descriptions that apply to almost everyone.

You are not as unique as you believe.
