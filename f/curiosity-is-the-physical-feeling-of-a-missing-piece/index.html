curiosity

Curiosity is the physical feeling of a missing piece.

A 1994 analysis defined curiosity as a 'cognitively induced deprivation' triggered by the awareness of a gap in one's knowledge.

The theory is titled the 'Information Gap Theory,' not a theory of cognitive dissonance; the author uses the latter only as a point of comparison for the resulting motivational state.

You are not looking for facts; you are looking for closure.

Where this comes from

Study
Loewenstein, 1994, Psychological Bulletin
What the check found
The source and year are correct, but the claim misidentifies the core definition of the 'Information Gap Theory' by conflating it with the distinct construct of cognitive dissonance.
