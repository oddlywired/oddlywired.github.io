memory

You struggle to remember joyful moments while feeling sad.

Mood-congruent memory is the phenomenon where your current emotional state dictates the accessibility of your past memories.

When you are in a negative mood, your brain is more likely to retrieve negative memories, making it difficult to shift your emotional state. This cycle reinforces your current feelings by filtering your past through a matching lens. Your internal state acts as a retrieval key for specific memories.

Your current mood limits the information you can access about your own life.
