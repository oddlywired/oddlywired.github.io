happiness

Your mood depends entirely on what you track.

Nothing in life is as important as you think it is while you are currently thinking about it.

The focusing illusion explains why people overestimate the impact of specific factors, like climate or income, on their overall happiness. By fixating on one variable, the brain ignores the thousands of others that contribute to daily satisfaction.

You are currently overestimating the importance of your focus.

Where this comes from

Study
Schkade and Kahneman, 1998
What the check found
