You struggle to change your mind even when presented with new facts.

A 2015 study found that repeated exposure to false claims makes them seem true, even when they contradict existing knowledge.

The study was conducted by Fazio et al. and published in the Journal of Experimental Psychology: General in 2015, not 2010.

Familiarity is often mistaken for accuracy.

What the check found
The findings described come from a 2015 study (Fazio et al.), not a 2010 study. The illusory truth effect has been documented since the late 1970s.
