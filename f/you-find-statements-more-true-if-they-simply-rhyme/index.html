You find statements more true if they simply rhyme.

Rhyming statements are processed as more accurate and truthful than non-rhyming statements.

A 2000 study demonstrated that aphorisms with rhyming patterns are perceived as more valid than identical ideas stated in non-rhyming formats. This happens because the brain experiences 'processing fluency' when a sentence flows easily, and it mistakes that ease for factual correctness. You equate aesthetic pleasure with cognitive truth.

You are easily swayed by how a sentence feels to say.
