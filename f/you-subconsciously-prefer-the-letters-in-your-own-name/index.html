You subconsciously prefer the letters in your own name.

While Pelham et al. (2002) published findings suggesting implicit egotism, those results have been widely criticized for failing to account for major confounding variables.

Subsequent studies (e.g., Simonsohn, 2011) demonstrated that the 'Dennis/Dentist' and 'George/Georgia' correlations vanish when controlling for age, gender, and socioeconomic trends, indicating no robust psychological preference.

Your environment often reflects your own identity more than you realize.

What the check found
The study exists, but the claimed effect has been largely debunked. Subsequent research showed the correlations were artifacts of confounding variables like age, birth year, and demographics rather than implicit egotism.
