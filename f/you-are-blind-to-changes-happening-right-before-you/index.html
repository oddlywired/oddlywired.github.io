You are blind to changes happening right before you.

In a 1998 study, about half of the participants failed to notice when the person they were talking to was swapped for another.

Simons, D. J., & Levin, D. T. (1998). Failure to detect changes to people during a real-world interaction. Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 5(4), 644–649.

Your mind paints a picture, but it leaves out most of the details.

Where this comes from

Study
Simons and Levin, 1998, Trends in Cognitive Sciences
What the check found
The study exists and the finding is accurate, but the source journal is incorrectly cited and the term 'most' implies a stronger majority than the 53% reported in the original paper.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
