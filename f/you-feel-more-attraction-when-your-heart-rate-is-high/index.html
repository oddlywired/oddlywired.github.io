You feel more attraction when your heart rate is high.

The misattribution of arousal causes you to interpret unrelated physical excitement as romantic interest.

In a 1974 study, men who crossed a high, anxiety-inducing suspension bridge were significantly more likely to call a female researcher for follow-up than those who crossed a low, stable bridge. The subjects mistook their adrenaline for attraction.

Your brain looks for a reason to explain why your body is racing.
