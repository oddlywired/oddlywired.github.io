You believe objects have a hidden true nature.

Psychological essentialism asserts that people believe certain categories—primarily natural kinds—possess an underlying, unobservable essence that determines their identity.

While Medin & Ortony (1989) established the theory, the 'original vs. replica' example is a later application of essentialist thinking to artifacts, not the foundational argument of that specific paper.

You attach meaning to the object, not the substance.

Where this comes from

Study
Medin & Ortony, 1989, Similarity and Analogical Reasoning
What the check found
Medin & Ortony defined psychological essentialism for natural kinds. Extending this to artifacts as a core proof is a secondary application of the theory, not the primary focus of the 1989 paper.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
