You believe every category has a hidden, unchanging truth.

Psychological essentialism explains your tendency to assume that groups—like gender or race—possess an immutable, internal 'essence'.

This happens regardless of the actual evidence for environmental or social influence. You treat superficial labels as if they identify a fundamental, biological, or moral category that cannot be altered, even when that belief is logically baseless.

Your mind tries to sort a complex world into rigid boxes.
