overclaiming

You claim to understand concepts that do not exist.

In a 2003 study, participants consistently claimed knowledge of nonexistent academic terms when asked to rate their familiarity with various topics.

Researchers tested participants on a mix of real and fabricated concepts. Many individuals rated their understanding of the fake terms as high, rather than admitting a lack of knowledge. This suggests a systemic tendency to over-report expertise to maintain a positive self-image.

Your brain prefers the appearance of knowing over the reality of ignorance.

Where this comes from

Study
Paulhus et al., 2003, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
What the check found
The study exists (Paulhus et al., 2003, JPSP) and establishes the Overclaiming Technique. It confirmed that people often claim familiarity with non-existent items to signal competence.
