You are more creative when you move your hands.

Gesturing while talking helps you organize your thought process.

Studies have shown that blocking hand movement during problem-solving tasks significantly decreases the quality of the solution. Gestures are not just for communicating to others; they are part of your own cognitive work.

Your body is an active partner in your thinking process.
