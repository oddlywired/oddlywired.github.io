attention

You focus on one problem and ignore the broader reality.

The focusing illusion causes you to overestimate the importance of a single factor on your overall well-being.

When you focus on a specific aspect of your life—like a new job or a current annoyance—that element consumes your attention, leading you to believe it dictates your total happiness. Once your attention shifts elsewhere, the impact of that factor on your satisfaction diminishes significantly. You confuse what you are thinking about with what matters most.

Nothing is as important as you think while you are thinking about it.
