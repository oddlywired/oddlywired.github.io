You subconsciously shift your persona for every setting.

Individuals who score high in self-monitoring intentionally adjust their behavior to match the expectations of their immediate social environment.

These people treat social situations as performances, constantly observing social cues to adapt their speech and actions. This happens so fluidly that the adjustment feels like a natural extension of who they are in that moment.

Your identity is a chameleon adapting to its container.

Where this comes from

Study
Snyder, 1974, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
What the check found
The claim accurately captures the core function of the self-monitoring construct as defined in Snyder's 1974 study, despite 'intentionally' being a slightly loaded characterization of what is often a fluid social skill.
