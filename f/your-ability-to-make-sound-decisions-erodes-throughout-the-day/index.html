decision making

Your ability to make sound decisions erodes throughout the day.

A 2011 study of over 1,000 judicial rulings found that parole boards were significantly more likely to grant favorable outcomes after a food break.

As the hours since the last meal increased, the likelihood of a positive decision dropped, regardless of the severity of the case. The brain treats complex decision-making as a high-energy activity that requires replenishment.

Your willpower is tied to your biology.
