motivation

You spend more time on tasks that offer no reward.

A 2006 study by Kivetz, Urminsky, and Zheng found that people persist with a task longer if they believe they are making progress, even if the progress is fake (the 'illusionary progress effect').

Kivetz, Urminsky, and Zheng (2006), Journal of Marketing Research

Your motivation is a reaction to perceived progress, not actual distance.
