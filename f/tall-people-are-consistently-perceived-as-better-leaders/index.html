Tall people are consistently perceived as better leaders.

A 2013 study found that taller U.S. presidential candidates were more likely to win a larger share of the popular vote, though they were not more likely to win the election itself.

The study analyzed U.S. presidential candidates rather than general workplace leadership perception.

Your visual assessment of competence is often just a reaction to someone's stature.

Where this comes from

Study
Stulp et al., 2013, PLOS ONE
What the check found
The cited study exists and is correctly attributed, but it addresses presidential popularity, not broad professional leadership perception.
