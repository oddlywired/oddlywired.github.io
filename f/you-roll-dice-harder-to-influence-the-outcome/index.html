control

You roll dice harder to influence the outcome.

People believe they have influence over random outcomes when they are personally involved in the process.

This is the illusion of control. In experiments, people wagered more money on lottery tickets they selected themselves versus those assigned to them, as if their choice gave them mastery over the laws of probability.

Agency is often confused with luck.
