You believe you know others better than they you.

A 2001 study showed that people consistently rate their understanding of others as superior to the understanding others have of them.

You can see the internal motivations behind your own behavior, but you only see the surface-level actions of others. This disparity leads to the conviction that you are an open book to them, while they remain a puzzle to you.

You hold the key to your own mind, but only observe the walls of others.

Where this comes from

Pronin, Kruger, Savitsky, and Ross, 2001
