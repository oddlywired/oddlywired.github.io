bias

You remember your contributions as disproportionately large.

Egocentric bias causes you to overstate your personal impact on group outcomes.

You have constant access to your own thoughts and efforts, but only limited access to others'. When recalling a project, your brain prioritizes your own internal experience. This leads to the consistent feeling that you did more work than your teammates.

Your memory of contribution is inherently skewed.
