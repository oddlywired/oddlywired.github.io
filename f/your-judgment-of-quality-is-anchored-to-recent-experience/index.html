perception

Your judgment of quality is anchored to recent experience.

The contrast effect is a established psychological phenomenon where perceptions are influenced by preceding, contrasting stimuli.

Kenrick and Gutierres (1980) demonstrated this effect in the specific context of ratings of human physical attractiveness, not in the appraisal of commercial products.

You rarely evaluate anything in total isolation.

What the check found
