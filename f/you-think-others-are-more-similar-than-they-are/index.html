You think others are more similar than they are.

Individuals consistently perceive members of their own group as highly diverse, while viewing outsiders as a uniform, homogenous group.

Outgroup homogeneity stems from the limited access we have to the personal lives of people outside our social circle. We have extensive data on our friends' complexities, but only categorical data on those we consider 'others'.

Your empathy is limited by your access to data.
