You struggle to accept information that changes your mind.

Motivated skepticism is the tendency to scrutinize counter-attitudinal information more intensely than information that supports your existing views.

When presented with data, people work significantly harder to find flaws in evidence that contradicts their beliefs. They apply a 'can I believe this?' test to evidence they dislike, while applying a 'must I believe this?' test to evidence they like. This creates an asymmetric processing of information that keeps beliefs intact despite contradictory input.

You are not looking for the truth; you are looking for confirmation.
