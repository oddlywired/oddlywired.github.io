cognition

You ignore information that contradicts your existing world view.

Confirmation bias is the tendency to search for, interpret, and recall information in a way that aligns with your pre-existing beliefs.

When you encounter new information, you selectively weigh evidence that supports your current position while scrutinizing or ignoring data that challenges it. This creates a self-reinforcing loop where your original views become hardened regardless of the quality of new evidence. You are looking for confirmation, not the truth.

Your brain is a filter, not a mirror.
