You feel an ownership for groups you did not join.

Basking in reflected glory is the tendency to associate yourself with successful groups or individuals to bolster your own self-image.

People are more likely to wear team apparel or use pronouns like 'we' following a victory rather than a loss. This serves as a psychological strategy to externalize success, allowing you to reap the social benefits of an achievement you did not contribute to. It is a subtle way of curating how you appear to others.

You borrow prestige to define your identity.
