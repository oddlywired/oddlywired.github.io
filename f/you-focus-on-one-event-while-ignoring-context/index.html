You focus on one event while ignoring context.

A 2000 study showed that people overestimate the impact of a single focal event on their overall happiness.

When asked how they would feel if their favorite team won a game, fans predicted an intense, long-lasting joy. However, after the win, their happiness returned to baseline quickly because they failed to account for the many other daily events that also dictate their mood.

Your focus narrows your perception of reality.
