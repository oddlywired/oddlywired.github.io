social dynamics

You define your worth by measuring it against others.

A 1954 study proposed that in the absence of objective standards, humans evaluate their abilities through social comparison.

You do not judge your performance against a vacuum; you look at the person next to you. This tendency forces you to constantly re-evaluate your own success based on the shifting status of your peers.

Your own bar is set by the person standing beside you.
