attribution

You rate people as smarter based on their role.

A 1977 study found that participants rated 'questioners' in a quiz show as more intelligent than 'contestants'.

The questioners chose the topics they were knowledgeable about, creating an illusion of superior intelligence that observers attributed to inherent trait ability. The participants failed to account for the situational advantage given to the person who controlled the questions.

You confuse situational power with intellectual merit.
