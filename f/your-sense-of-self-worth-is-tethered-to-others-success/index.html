social dynamics

Your sense of self-worth is tethered to others' success.

Individuals prefer to evaluate their abilities using objective standards when available, turning to social comparison only when such objective means are lacking.

Festinger (1954) identified social comparison as a response to the unavailability of objective, nonsocial means of evaluation.

Your progress feels like failure when measured against the wrong yardstick.

Where this comes from

Study
Festinger (1954)
What the check found
The claim misrepresents the hierarchy of Festinger's theory; objective evaluation remains the preferred method when possible.
