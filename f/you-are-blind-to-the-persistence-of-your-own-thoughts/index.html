You are blind to the persistence of your own thoughts.

The harder you attempt to suppress a specific thought, the more frequently it enters your awareness.

Suppression requires you to monitor your mind for the presence of that thought. This monitoring process keeps the thought active in your working memory. The very mechanism designed to banish the idea ensures it remains at the front of your consciousness.

You cannot stop thinking about a secret without constantly reminding yourself what that secret is.
