embodied cognition

Holding a warm drink makes you judge others as warmer.

A 2008 study by Williams and Bargh reported this effect, but it has not held up under independent replication.

Three independent studies with larger sample sizes failed to replicate the original finding, suggesting the effect may be smaller, non-existent, or highly context-dependent.

Your social evaluations are tethered to your physical environment.
