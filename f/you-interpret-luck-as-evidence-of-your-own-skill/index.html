control

You interpret luck as evidence of your own skill.

People who perceive a sense of control over a random event significantly overestimate their probability of success.

In a 1975 study, participants who chose their own lottery ticket were less willing to sell it than those who were assigned one, treating chance as a performance-based task.

You mistake your involvement for your influence.
