You remember facts better if you explain them yourself.

The generation effect demonstrates that information is better retained when you produce it from your own mind.

A 1978 study found that participants recalled words better if they generated them based on a rule rather than simply reading them on a list.

Passive consumption is the enemy of actual retention.

Where this comes from

Study
Slamecka and Graf, 1978, Journal of Experimental Psychology
What the check found
The core claim is accurate and widely accepted in cognitive psychology. The citation is correct, though the journal title in 1978 was specifically 'Journal of Experimental Psychology: Human Learning and Memory'.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
