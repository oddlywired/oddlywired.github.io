anonymity

You are more likely to lie when you feel anonymous.

Deindividuation reduces the internalized social norms that typically constrain honesty.

In studies involving dimmed lights or masks, participants cheated significantly more on tasks to earn extra money. Anonymity masks the surveillance of social standards, allowing the brain to bypass self-regulatory mechanisms designed to maintain reputation.

You are only as moral as the people watching you.

Where this comes from

Study
Zhong, Bohns, and Gino, 2010, Psychological Science
What the check found
The cited study, 'Good Lamps Are the Best Police: Darkness Increases Dishonesty and Self-Interested Behavior' (2010), confirms both the findings regarding darkness/masks and the mechanism of perceived anonymity.
Confidence
Well replicated
