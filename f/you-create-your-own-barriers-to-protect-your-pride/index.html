You create your own barriers to protect your pride.

Self-handicapping is the psychological strategy of placing obstacles in your own path to provide an excuse for future failure.

By staying up late or not preparing for a task, you ensure that if you fail, you have a ready-made external reason. If you succeed despite the obstacle, your achievement appears even more impressive to others and yourself.

You would rather fail by your own hand than by your limitations.
