Theory of Mind is a developmental process that matures throughout early childhood, with performance on false-belief tasks typically improving significantly around age four.

Success in false-belief tasks is highly dependent on how the task is presented, such as the use of non-verbal cues or changes in task complexity, which can lead to success at younger ages.
