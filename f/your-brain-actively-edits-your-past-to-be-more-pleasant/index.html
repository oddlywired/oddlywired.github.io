Your brain actively edits your past to be more pleasant.

Participants in a 1997 study reported that their past vacations were significantly more enjoyable than they had rated them during the actual trip.

Rosy retrospection describes how our memories strip away minor irritations and inconveniences over time. You are left with a highlight reel that feels like reality.

You are living in a slightly fictionalized version of your own history.

Where this comes from

Study
Mitchell, Thompson, and Peterson, 1997
What the check found
The study by Mitchell et al. (1997) is a well-established foundational paper for the phenomenon known as 'rosy retrospection'.
