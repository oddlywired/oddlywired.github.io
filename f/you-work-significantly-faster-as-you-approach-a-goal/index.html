motivation

You work significantly faster as you approach a goal.

The goal gradient effect shows that the closer you are to finishing a task, the more effort you invest to complete it.

In a 2006 study, participants with digital stamp cards were more likely to use them as they neared the goal of a free item. The perception of progress increases your motivation more than the distance remaining.

You are most efficient when you can see the finish line.

Where this comes from

Study
Kivetz, Urminsky, and Zheng, 2006, The Goal Gradient Effect
