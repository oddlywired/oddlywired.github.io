You blame the victim to feel safer yourself.

People often assume that victims of misfortune deserve their fate to maintain a belief in a just world.

This cognitive bias suggests that the world is a fair place where people get what they earn. When you see someone suffer, the discomfort of the situation leads you to find flaws in their actions to justify why they deserved the outcome.

Fairness is a narrative, not a state of reality.
