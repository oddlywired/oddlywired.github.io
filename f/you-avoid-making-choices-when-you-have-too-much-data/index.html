You avoid making choices when you have too much data.

A 2000 study by Iyengar and Lepper suggested that, in specific conditions, limited choice sets can lead to higher conversion rates than extensive ones.

The original study observed that among those who stopped at a display, 30% bought from a 6-jam set compared to 3% from a 24-jam set; however, this effect has not been consistently replicated in later studies.

You are not looking for the perfect option; you are looking for simplicity.
