You are a slave to your previous assumptions.

Schema-based processing leads to faster information integration at the cost of detail.

When we encounter a new situation, we use a pre-existing schema to organize expectations. If we encounter information that fits the schema, we process it rapidly and remember the core concept. If we encounter contradictory data, we often unconsciously warp it to fit our schema or ignore it entirely.

Your world is built from the templates you already hold.
