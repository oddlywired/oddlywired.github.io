You rewrite your memory to claim you saw it coming.

Fischhoff (1975) demonstrated that after an event occurs, people consistently overestimate their ability to have predicted the outcome.

Once a fact is known, your brain instantly organizes your memory to fit the new information, making the event feel inevitable. This leaves you feeling as though your past self was much more perceptive than they actually were.

You are a master at tricking yourself into believing you knew the future.

Where this comes from

Study
Baruch Fischhoff, 1975, Journal of Experimental Psychology
What the check found
Fischhoff (1975) is the seminal study identifying 'hindsight bias.' The citation is accurate and the described effect is well-established in psychology.
