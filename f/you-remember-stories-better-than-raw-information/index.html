narrative

You remember stories better than raw information.

A 1969 study by Bower and Clark showed that readers remembered information significantly better when it was framed as a narrative.

The study was published in 1969, not 1980.

Your memory is not a database but a library of narratives.

Where this comes from

Study
Bower and Clark (1980)
What the check found
The claim correctly identifies the authors but misattributes the year of the study. The effect is well-documented in psychology but the specific 1980 date is incorrect.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
