You overestimate how much the world focuses on you.

The spotlight effect describes your tendency to believe that your appearance and actions are more visible to others than they really are.

A 2000 study showed that people wearing conspicuous clothing drastically overestimated how many onlookers would notice them. People are usually too occupied with their own internal narrative to watch yours.

The world is not watching, but you are.
