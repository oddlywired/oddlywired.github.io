morality

Doing one good deed makes you act more selfishly.

Moral licensing occurs when performing a virtuous action provides a psychological credit that you spend on later behavior.

In a 2001 study, participants who were given the opportunity to show they were not prejudiced were subsequently more likely to express biased views. Once you view yourself as a 'good person' in one area, you feel subconsciously permitted to act in ways that contradict those values later. You track your morality like a bank account.

Your past goodness can justify your future lapses.
