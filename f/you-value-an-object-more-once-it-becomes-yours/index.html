You value an object more once it becomes yours.

The endowment effect shows that ownership increases your valuation of an item, even if you acquired it moments ago.

In a 1990 experiment, participants were given a mug and then asked the price at which they would sell it. They consistently demanded twice as much as others were willing to pay to buy the same item.

Your attachment to things often blinds you to their market value.
