You feel an unconscious pressure to justify social fairness.

People consistently defend the legitimacy of the status quo to maintain psychological stability and a sense of control.

Research indicates that even those who are disadvantaged by a current system often perceive it as fair and legitimate. This cognitive drive minimizes the discomfort of inequality by rationalizing existing power structures as necessary or deserved.

We prefer believing the world is just over facing its randomness.
