planning

You are systematically wrong about your own schedule.

The planning fallacy is the tendency to underestimate the time needed to complete a future task.

A 1994 study found that students consistently predicted they would finish their thesis weeks earlier than they actually did. Even when they had experienced previous delays, they ignored their own history and assumed this time would be different.

Your past failures to meet deadlines do not inform your future predictions.
