You take the credit and blame the world.

A 1975 study by Miller and Ross examined the self-serving bias, questioning whether the attribution of success to internal factors and failure to external ones is a defense mechanism or a byproduct of information processing.

The study was titled 'Self-serving biases in the attribution of causality: Fact or fiction?' and challenged the prevailing assumption that these attributions are exclusively ego-protective.

You are the hero of your own story and the victim of your own surroundings.
