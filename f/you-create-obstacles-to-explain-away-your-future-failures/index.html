You create obstacles to explain away your future failures.

Self-handicapping occurs when you intentionally place hurdles in your own path to protect your ego.

If you perform poorly after staying up all night, you can blame your lack of sleep rather than your lack of ability. This preserves your self-esteem by shifting the cause of a potential failure to an external factor.

You would rather fail because of an excuse than risk failing because of yourself.
