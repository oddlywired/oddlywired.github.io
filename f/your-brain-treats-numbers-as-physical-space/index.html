Your brain treats numbers as physical space.

The SNARC effect reveals that humans associate smaller numbers with the left side of space and larger numbers with the right.

In a 1993 study, participants responded faster to small numbers when clicking with their left hand and large numbers with their right. This internal spatial line suggests that your numerical processing is deeply linked to your motor system.

You are literally mapping quantities onto your physical world.
