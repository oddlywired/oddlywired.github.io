planning

Your best-case scenario is your default timeline for tasks.

People consistently underestimate the time, costs, and risks of future projects.

You focus on your personal vision of the future rather than historical data on how long similar tasks actually take. This creates a persistent gap between your projected and actual progress. Experience rarely corrects your future estimates.

You remain optimistic about your own efficiency.
