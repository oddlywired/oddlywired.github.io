optimism

You expect good things to happen to you disproportionately.

The optimism bias is a cognitive illusion where you believe you are less at risk of negative events and more likely to experience positive outcomes than your peers.

While this bias helps maintain resilience, it leads to the systemic underestimation of risks in finances, health, and career. It is a fundamental feature of the human brain's focus on future-directed goals.

You are the exception to every rule you see for others.
