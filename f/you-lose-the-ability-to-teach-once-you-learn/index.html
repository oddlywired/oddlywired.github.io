You lose the ability to teach once you learn.

The curse of knowledge describes the inability to imagine that other people do not know what you know.

In a 1990 experiment, 'tappers' were asked to tap a song on a table. They predicted 50% of listeners would guess the song correctly, but because the tappers could hear the melody in their own heads, only 2.5% of listeners succeeded.

Once you have grasped a concept, the perspective of a beginner becomes invisible to you.
