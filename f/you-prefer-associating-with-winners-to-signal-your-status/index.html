You prefer associating with winners to signal your status.

A 1976 study found students wore more university gear the day after their football team won.

By highlighting the success of others, you subconsciously attempt to bolster your own perceived competence. You treat the success of a group as your own property.

You gain social validation by proximity to success, not just by earning it.
