You judge a stranger's intelligence by their physical attractiveness.

The halo effect is a cognitive bias where one trait influences the overall perception of a person, which Thorndike first observed in workplace/military performance ratings.

Thorndike's 1920 study analyzed how positive or negative ratings in one professional trait influenced ratings in unrelated professional categories, rather than proving that appearance alone dictates the perception of personality traits.

You are judging a book by its cover while believing you are reading the pages.

Where this comes from

Study
Edward Thorndike, 1920
What the check found
Thorndike coined 'halo effect' in 1920, but the claim oversimplifies his study on general performance ratings into a specific theory about appearance.

Solid, with limits
