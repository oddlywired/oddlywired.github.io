decision making

Your best decisions happen when you stop thinking hard.

A 2006 study by Dijksterhuis proposed that complex decisions are made more effectively when distracted, though these findings have faced extensive challenges in replication.

The 'unconscious thought effect' is widely debated and remains controversial, with many contemporary studies finding no consistent evidence for its existence or practical application.

Some of your best work happens when you walk away.
