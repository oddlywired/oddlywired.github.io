social influence

You are more honest when you feel observed by eyes.

A simple image of eyes displayed in a room increases prosocial behavior and honesty in participants.

In a 2006 field experiment, researchers placed a poster of human eyes above an office honesty box. Contributions increased significantly because the brain is hardwired to respond to social monitoring, even when that monitoring is non-human.

You behave as if you are watched, even in private.
