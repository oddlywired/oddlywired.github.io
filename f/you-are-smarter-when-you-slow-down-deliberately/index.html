You are smarter when you slow down deliberately.

The Cognitive Reflection Test predicts performance on logical tasks by measuring impulse inhibition.

Participants presented with 'trick' questions often answer with the intuitive response first, despite it being mathematically incorrect. Those who pause to override this initial impulse consistently score higher on unrelated tasks involving complex decision-making. It reveals that intelligence is partially the ability to stop your first thought.

Your gut feeling is frequently your biggest cognitive obstacle.
