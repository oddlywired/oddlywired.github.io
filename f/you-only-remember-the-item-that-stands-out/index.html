You only remember the item that stands out.

The Von Restorff effect describes a mnemonic advantage where unique or distinctive items within a series of similar items are more likely to be remembered, likely due to increased distinctiveness or specialized categorization.

Remove the claim that the brain prioritizes these items to conserve resources for potential threats or anomalies.

You ignore the pattern and obsess over the exception.

Where this comes from

Study
Von Restorff, 1933, Psychologische Forschung
What the check found
The core psychological phenomenon is accurate, but the explanation regarding resource conservation for 'threats' is an unverified, speculative hypothesis that is not part of the documented effect.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
