The scent of your environment dictates your ability to focus.

A 2003 study found that exposure to peppermint odor significantly improves performance on tasks requiring sustained attention.

Researchers measured participants' alertness and memory speed after being exposed to subtle peppermint scents in a laboratory setting. The results indicated that the scent acts as a physiological trigger that modulates arousal levels. It helps maintain engagement during repetitive tasks that usually induce mental fatigue.

You can sometimes hack your concentration through your olfactory system.
