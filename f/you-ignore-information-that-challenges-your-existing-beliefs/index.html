bias

You ignore information that challenges your existing beliefs.

A 1979 study found that participants discounted evidence that contradicted their opinions while accepting flawed evidence that supported them.

This is confirmation bias. We actively seek out data that confirms what we already think and disregard anything that makes us reconsider. It is a protective mechanism for our current internal narrative.

You are not looking for the truth; you are looking for verification.

Where this comes from

Study
Lord, Ross, and Lepper, 1979
