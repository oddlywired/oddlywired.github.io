identity

You change your attitude to match your behavior.

Self-perception theory suggests that when your internal beliefs are unclear, you infer them by observing your own past actions.

A 1967 study found that people who were paid to express a specific opinion later adopted that opinion as their own. We act like observers of our own lives, building our identities from the outside in.

You become what you choose to do.
