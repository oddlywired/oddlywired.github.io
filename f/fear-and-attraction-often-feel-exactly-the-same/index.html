Fear and attraction often feel exactly the same.

A 1974 study found that men were significantly more likely to call a female interviewer after crossing a scary suspension bridge.

The high physiological arousal from fear was misinterpreted as intense romantic interest. Your brain often assigns the wrong source to your current physical state.

Your feelings are often just a guess about your body's chemistry.

Where this comes from

Dutton and Aron, 1974, Misattribution of Arousal
