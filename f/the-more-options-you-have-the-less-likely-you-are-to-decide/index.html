decision making

The more options you have, the less likely you are to decide.

In a famous 2000 study, shoppers were 10 times more likely to purchase jam when presented with 6 options rather than 24.

This is choice overload. When the number of options exceeds your processing capacity, your brain experiences decision paralysis. You prioritize avoiding the anxiety of a wrong choice over the benefit of the best choice.

A lack of choice is often a hidden productivity tool.

Where this comes from

Study
Iyengar and Lepper (2000)
What the check found
The math (30% vs 3% = 10x) is accurate to the original study's findings as cited in the reference material, even if the general effect of choice overload is scientifically contested.
