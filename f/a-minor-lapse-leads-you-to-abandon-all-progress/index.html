A minor lapse leads you to abandon all progress.

The what-the-hell effect explains why one small deviation from a goal often results in a total collapse of self-control.

Once you feel you have 'broken' your restriction, the psychological distress makes you stop caring about further consequences. You treat the initial failure as a signal that the goal is already lost.

The harm is rarely in the single mistake, but in your reaction to it.
