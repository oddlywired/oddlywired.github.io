You assume that people act according to their nature.

The correspondence bias is the tendency to explain behavior as a function of personality rather than the situational constraints.

If you see a stranger snap at a waiter, you assume they are an aggressive person. You rarely pause to consider that they might be having a crisis, because the internal label is faster and requires less cognitive effort.

You define people by their actions, but yourself by your circumstances.
