motivation

You prioritize small gains over large rewards.

The 'present bias' is an economic model describing the tendency to value smaller, immediate rewards over larger, delayed ones.

The evolutionary claim regarding resource acquisition is a theoretical hypothesis for why this bias exists, not a confirmed fact or a finding from the cited O'Donoghue & Rabin paper.

Your biological hardware is programmed to favor the immediate over the optimal.

Where this comes from

Study
O'Donoghue & Rabin, 1999, The Quarterly Journal of Economics
What the check found
The economic definition of the bias is accurate, but the causal evolutionary explanation provided is speculative and not part of the cited academic research.
