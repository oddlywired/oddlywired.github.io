social perception

You assume attractive people possess other positive traits.

The halo effect causes people to perceive physically appealing individuals as more intelligent and honest.

In a 1972 study, researchers found that participants rated attractive subjects as having more socially desirable personalities without any supporting evidence. This bias occurs instantly and operates outside of conscious awareness.

We conflate visual beauty with internal virtue.

Where this comes from

Study
Dion et al., 1972
What the check found
The seminal 1972 study identified a broad positive bias toward attractive individuals, which subsequent research has consistently mapped to specific traits like intelligence and honesty.
