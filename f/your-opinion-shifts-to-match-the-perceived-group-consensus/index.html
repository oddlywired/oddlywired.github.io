conformity

Your opinion shifts to match the perceived group consensus.

A 1951 study showed that participants would give a clearly incorrect answer just to align with the rest of the group.

Social pressure creates a desire for cohesion that overrides the accuracy of our own senses. Even when the group is clearly wrong, individuals often doubt their own perception to avoid being the outlier. We are biologically predisposed to prioritize belonging over objective truth.

You will trade your accuracy for the safety of being one of the group.

Where this comes from

Study
Asch, 1951, Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology
What the check found
