Bad news is heavier than good news.

A 2008 review found that humans process negative stimuli faster, remember them longer, and react to them more intensely.

The mind is designed to prioritize survival over happiness. A single insult requires more cognitive effort to process than a compliment, explaining why one bad review outweighs dozens of good ones.

You are biologically biased to notice the threat in the room.
