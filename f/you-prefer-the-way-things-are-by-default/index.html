You prefer the way things are by default.

The status quo bias describes the tendency to maintain a current state because the potential losses of changing are perceived as greater than the benefits, a concept driven by loss aversion.

Change the focus from 'simply because changing requires effort' to 'because potential losses are weighted more heavily than potential gains'.

Your current life is a product of inertia.

Where this comes from

Study
Samuelson and Zeckhauser, 1988
What the check found
The claim oversimplifies the mechanism. It isn't just about 'effort' or 'laziness,' but about the cognitive asymmetry where potential losses feel more impactful than potential gains.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
