Reflecting on your values can undo your defensiveness.

A 1988 study found that when people wrote about a personal value, they were more likely to accept information that previously threatened their self-view.

Self-affirmation theory demonstrates that when you feel secure about your core identity, you no longer need to distort facts to defend your ego.

You become more rational when you feel secure about who you are.
