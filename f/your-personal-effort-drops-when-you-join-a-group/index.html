Your personal effort drops when you join a group.

The phenomenon of decreased group effort is often termed 'social loafing,' a term coined by Latané et al. in 1979, which is historically rooted in the 'Ringelmann effect' documented in 1913.

Ringelmann's 1913 paper identified performance deficits in groups, but the specific term 'social loafing' post-dates his publication.

Your motivation is tied to the visibility of your individual contribution.
