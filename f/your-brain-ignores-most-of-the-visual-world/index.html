Your brain ignores most of the visual world.

Change blindness results suggest that visual perception is not a continuous, high-definition recording, highlighting the dependency of visual awareness on focused attention.

The 1998 study empirically demonstrated the limits of human change detection during real-world interactions rather than defining the brain's data-processing architecture.

Your perception of reality is a highlight reel, not a recording.

Where this comes from

Study
Simons, D. J., & Levin, D. T., 1998, 'Failure to detect changes to people during a real-world interaction'
What the check found
The study confirms the phenomenon of change blindness but does not articulate the neurological mechanism of 'low-resolution mapping'. It is a behavioral experiment, not a comprehensive theory of brain architecture.

Solid, with limits
