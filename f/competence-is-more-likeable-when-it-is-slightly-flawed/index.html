Competence is more likeable when it is slightly flawed.

The pratfall effect is the tendency for a mistake to increase the attractiveness of an already highly competent person, while a mistake by an average or incompetent person tends to make them less attractive.

The original study, Aronson, Willerman, and Floyd, was published in 1966, not 1968.

Flawlessness is not the trait that keeps people close to you.

Where this comes from

Study
Aronson, Willerman, and Floyd, 1966, The Pratfall Effect
What the check found
The effect is not universal; it is highly dependent on the subject's baseline competence. A blunder does not inherently make people more attractive.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
