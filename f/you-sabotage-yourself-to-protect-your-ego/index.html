You sabotage yourself to protect your ego.

A 1978 study found that participants chose to consume performance-inhibiting drugs when they were anxious about an upcoming test.

By creating obstacles for yourself, you ensure an external excuse for potential failure. If you succeed, you are a genius; if you fail, you have a ready-made reason that isn't your own ability.

You prefer failure to the threat of incompetence.

Where this comes from

Study
Berglas and Jones, 1978
What the check found
The study is correctly attributed. The claim simplifies the motivation from 'fear of failing to repeat success' to 'anxiety about an upcoming test', which is a common and minor distillation.
Confidence
Well replicated
