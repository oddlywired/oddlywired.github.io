Evidence against your belief makes you hold tighter.

Belief perseverance is the cognitive tendency to maintain a position even when the evidence supporting it has been clearly debunked.

Once you have formed an explanation for why a belief is true, that explanation becomes an independent structure in your mind. Even when the original source is removed, the structure persists and protects itself from contradictory information.

You do not hold your beliefs; your beliefs hold you.

Where this comes from

Study
Ross, Lepper, and Hubbard, 1975, Belief Perseverance
