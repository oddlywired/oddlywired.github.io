You value your possessions simply because you own them.

People demand more money to give up an item than they would have paid to acquire it.

In a 1990 study, students were given a mug and then asked the price at which they would sell it. Sellers set prices significantly higher than the buyers, who were only willing to pay a fraction of that amount. Ownership changes the way you perceive the value of the object.

You are biased toward keeping what you already hold.
