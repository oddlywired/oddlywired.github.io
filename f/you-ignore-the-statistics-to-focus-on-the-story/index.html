You ignore the statistics to focus on the story.

People disregard general statistical information in favor of specific, anecdotal evidence.

When given a description of a person and asked for their profession, individuals ignore population ratios. Instead, they rely on how well the description fits a stereotype, causing systematic errors in judgment.

A single compelling detail carries more weight for you than a thousand dry facts.
