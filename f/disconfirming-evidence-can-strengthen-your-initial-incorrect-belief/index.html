Disconfirming evidence can strengthen your initial incorrect belief.

The 'backfire effect' was proposed in 2010 to describe people doubling down on beliefs, but subsequent large-scale research has failed to replicate this as a common phenomenon.

The backfire effect is currently not considered a well-established or common psychological occurrence.
