problem solving

You struggle to solve problems you have used before.

Functional fixedness is a cognitive bias where you become unable to see an object as having any function other than its original one.

When participants were given a box and a candle, they struggled to use the box as a platform if it was previously filled with tacks, seeing it only as a container.

Your past experience creates your current limitations.

Where this comes from

Study
Karl Duncker, 1945
What the check found
The definition and attribution are accurate within the standards of social psychology, though the 1945 date is an English-language citation for work originally published in German in 1935.
