projection

You overestimate how your future self will feel.

The projection bias causes you to project your current tastes or preferences onto your future decisions.

Projection bias refers to the misprediction of future tastes (like hunger) rather than emotional states; the latter is typically categorized under impact bias within affective forecasting.

Your present mood is an unreliable narrator for your future needs.

Where this comes from

Study
Loewenstein et al., 2003
What the check found
