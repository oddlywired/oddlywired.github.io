Your brain shuts down for half a second after focusing.

Participants show significantly reduced performance in detecting a second target when it appears 200 to 500 milliseconds after the first.

The effect is not a total inability to detect the target, and it generally does not occur for targets appearing immediately (within approximately 100ms) after the first.

You are functionally blind to the world for a fraction of every second.

Where this comes from

Study
Raymond, Shapiro, and Arnell, 1992, Journal of Experimental Psychology
What the check found
The blink is a performance dip, not a total inability, and is specifically observed between 200 and 500ms, not across the entire 0-500ms window.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
