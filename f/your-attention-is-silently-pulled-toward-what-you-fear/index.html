attention

Your attention is silently pulled toward what you fear.

An attentional bias causes the mind to demonstrate an initial orientation toward threat-related stimuli before conscious awareness.

Change 'scan for and dwell on' to 'demonstrate an initial orientation toward'.

You are always looking for trouble, whether you realize it or not.

Where this comes from

Study
MacLeod et al. (1986) in Journal of Abnormal Psychology
What the check found
The study confirms initial attentional bias toward threat, but 'dwelling' implies a maintenance of attention that the original dot-probe task design was not primarily intended to measure.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
