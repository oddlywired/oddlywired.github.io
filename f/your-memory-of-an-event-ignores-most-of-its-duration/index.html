Your memory of an event ignores most of its duration.

The peak intensity and the final moments of an experience define your entire retrospective evaluation.

In a 1993 study, participants chose a longer, less painful procedure over a shorter, more intense one because the end was less severe. We do not aggregate the total experience; we sample the extremes and the conclusion.

You are judging your life based on a highlight reel.
