Performing a simple ritual lowers your anxiety before high-stakes tasks.

Research shows that simple, repetitive ritualistic behaviors can reduce physiological distress before a performance, particularly for those experiencing high levels of performance anxiety.

The benefit of the ritual is often dependent on the individual's baseline state, rather than occurring uniformly regardless of baseline anxiety.

Small, meaningless actions can serve as effective anchors for your focus.

Where this comes from

Study
Brooks et al., 2016, Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes
What the check found
The study by Brooks et al. (2016) exists and supports the core premise, but the assertion that the effect is independent of baseline anxiety levels is an overgeneralization of the study's specific findings.
Confidence
Solid, with limits
