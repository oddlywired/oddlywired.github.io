creativity

You are more creative when your physical environment feels expansive.

A 2007 study by Meyers-Levy and Zhu in the Journal of Consumer Research showed that ceiling height influences whether individuals engage in abstract, relational thinking or concrete, detail-oriented thinking.

The research dates to 2007 and observes a shift in cognitive processing style rather than an inherent increase in creative performance.

Your physical environment is dictating the breadth of your ideas.

Where this comes from

Study
Meyers-Levy and Zhu, 2012, Journal of Consumer Research
What the check found
The study is correctly identified as Meyers-Levy and Zhu (2007), not 2012. It describes a change in processing style, not a blanket improvement in creative performance.

Solid, with limits
