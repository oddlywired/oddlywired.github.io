Darkness changes the way you think.

A 2013 study found that dimmed ambient lighting significantly boosts creative performance compared to bright light settings.

Researchers observed that lower light levels reduce the inhibition of abstract thinking by creating a psychological sense of freedom. Participants in dimly lit rooms showed greater flexibility in generating creative solutions.

Environment sets the boundaries for your mental state.
