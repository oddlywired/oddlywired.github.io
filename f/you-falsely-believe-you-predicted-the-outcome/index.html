memory

You falsely believe you predicted the outcome.

Hindsight bias causes you to view events as inevitable after they have occurred.

Once you know the result of a decision, your memory reconfigures to suggest you saw it coming all along. This prevents you from learning from errors because you convince yourself your logic was sound from the start.

We rewrite our past to maintain an illusion of foresight.
