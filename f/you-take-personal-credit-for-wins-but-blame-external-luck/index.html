self perception

You take personal credit for wins but blame external luck.

The self-serving bias is the tendency to attribute success to your internal character and failure to your environment.

When you succeed at a task, you view it as evidence of your ability. When you fail at the same task, you view it as a momentary error caused by the situation.

You are a master of creating narratives that protect your self-image.

Miller and Ross (1975)
