Ambient noise levels determine your creativity.

A 2012 study found that moderate levels of ambient noise boost abstract thinking compared to total silence.

Moderate noise creates a slight cognitive challenge, which encourages the brain to think more creatively to process information.

Silence may be too easy for your brain to engage with effectively.
