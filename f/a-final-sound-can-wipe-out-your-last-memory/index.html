A final sound can wipe out your last memory.

The suffix effect is the finding that recall of the final items in a list is inhibited if an irrelevant, spoken sound is presented immediately after the list ends.

If a person is asked to remember a sequence of numbers, the addition of a 'useless' syllable like 'zero' at the end significantly decreases their ability to report the final items.

The most recent information you receive is the most fragile.
