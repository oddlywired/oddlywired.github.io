You are more likely to lie after doing something ethical.

Moral licensing is the subconscious process where performing a good deed makes you feel entitled to act in your own self-interest later.

A 2010 study found that participants who were given the opportunity to show their moral integrity were more likely to later cheat on a task to gain a reward. Your brain keeps a mental ledger of your goodness, and once you have 'earned' enough, it resets the rules.

Your past virtues are the easiest excuse for your current vices.
