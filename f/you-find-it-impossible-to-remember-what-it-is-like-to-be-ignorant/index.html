You find it impossible to remember what it is like to be ignorant.

A 1989 study found that better-informed agents cannot accurately anticipate the judgments and market valuations of less-informed agents.

The research specifically tested bargaining and economic decision-making, not the pedagogical or cognitive difficulty of learning new information.

The more you know, the worse you are at teaching.
