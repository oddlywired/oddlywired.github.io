ego

You claim personal credit for wins but blame luck.

A 1978 study showed that participants took credit for success but attributed failure to external variables like test difficulty.

To maintain self-esteem, you internalize positive results as reflections of your ability while distancing yourself from negative results. This protects your ego, but obscures the reality of your performance.

You are your own most generous and least accurate judge.
