You are a member of a group just by existing.

A 1970 study showed that people favored others simply because they were assigned to the same arbitrary group, even when the groups had no meaning.

In the minimal group paradigm, people assigned to 'Group A' consistently gave more resources to fellow members than to 'Group B.' We are hardwired to distinguish between 'us' and 'them.' We prioritize our tribe even when the definition of that tribe is completely imaginary.

You are biased toward your group before you even know who they are.
