Writing your worries down fixes your focus.

In a 2011 study, college students who wrote about their anxieties before an exam performed significantly better than those who did not.

Writing acts as a cognitive outlet, offloading the memory burden of stressful thoughts. By externalizing the worry, you free up the mental capacity required for complex problem-solving.

Your brain performs better when it is not carrying the mental load.

Where this comes from

Study
Ramirez and Beilock, 2011, Science
What the check found
The study exists and confirms the claim, though it is specifically relevant to highly anxious students rather than the entire student population.
