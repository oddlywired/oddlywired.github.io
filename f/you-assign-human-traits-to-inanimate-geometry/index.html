anthropomorphism

You assign human traits to inanimate geometry.

In a 1944 study, viewers who watched an animation of shapes moving around a box almost universally described the shapes as having distinct, anthropomorphic personalities.

Your brain is hardwired to find agency and intention in motion. You cannot stop yourself from attributing motivations, such as aggression or fear, to objects that have none.

You are desperate to find a story in the chaos.

Where this comes from

Study
Heider and Simmel, 1944, The American Journal of Psychology
What the check found
The 1944 study by Heider and Simmel is a classic in psychology, and 33 of their 34 subjects attributed intent to the shapes, supporting the 'almost universal' claim.
