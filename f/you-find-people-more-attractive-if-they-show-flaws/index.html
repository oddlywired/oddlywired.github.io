You find people more attractive if they show flaws.

Highly competent individuals are often rated more favorably after making a minor, relatable blunder, while less competent individuals are typically rated less favorably.

The pratfall effect is dependent on initial perceived competence; a mistake does not universally increase attractiveness.

Your mistake might be the bridge that makes you relatable.

Where this comes from

Study
Aronson et al., 1966, Psychonomic Science
What the check found
The claim presents a conditional psychological finding as a universal positive, ignoring that the effect is negative for those not previously established as competent.
Confidence
Well replicated
